## ⚡ The Short Version

```text
Senior Flutter Developer · Lahore, Pakistan
4+ years turning fuzzy ideas into apps people use every day.
40+ apps live on the App Store and Google Play.
Open source author · Technical writer · Perpetual learner.
```

I studied Software Engineering at KFUEIT and spent the years since shipping
mobile products across industries — soil science, luxury hospitality, wellness,
food-tech, and more. I care deeply about the gap between spec and shipped:
how it feels on a real device, how it holds up with messy data, and how easy
it is for the next engineer to pick up.

Currently at **[Crewlogix Technologies](https://crewlogix.com)** · Growing into
**Node.js · NestJS · PostgreSQL** · Open to freelance & collaboration.
